# Operational runbook contracts

Status: Phase 0 procedures to implement and exercise; no live infrastructure or incident is implied. Sources: master §§59–61, 70–74 and 88. [Deployment architecture](DEPLOYMENT_ARCHITECTURE.md) defines recovery objectives; [observability](OBSERVABILITY.md) defines signals. Environment IDs, commands, provider contacts and on-call personnel must be filled with verified deployment facts in implementation, not invented here.

Every executed runbook records incident/change ID, operator/approver, scope, start/end, affected artifact/schema/provider versions, observed state, exact actions, sanitized evidence, outcome and follow-up owner. Use scoped temporary credentials and preserve evidence before mutation. Security and financial recovery require the owning domain's authorized commands.

| Runbook | Trigger / accountable owner | Procedure contract | Reopen / exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| RB-001 Dependency outage | Sustained API/DB/Redis/provider errors; SRE | Classify required versus optional dependency; protect accepted intents; disable unsafe writes; drain/retry bounded work; restore dependency and replay durable checkpoints | Dependency health plus no lost commands, duplicate effects or stuck unknown operations; optional AI outage does not disable ordinary deals |
| RB-002 Financial uncertainty | Unknown operation, mismatch, signature conflict; Settlement operations | Hold conflicting instructions; retain exact operation/key/account/environment; fetch authoritative provider activity; reconcile journal/destinations/approvals; assigned review resolves discrepancy through normal commands | Every movement explained; no duplicate liability; holds lifted only by authorized domain policy |
| RB-003 Document exposure | Unauthorized bytes/capability or private cache hit; Security + Documents | Stop new capability issuance; revoke affected grants/sessions; restrict object delivery; preserve access evidence; assess issued-link residual window and delivered copies; scoped notification/legal process | Policy and cache/object gateway negative tests; exposure scope and required notifications reviewed |
| RB-004 Staff/account compromise | Credential/session abuse or privilege anomaly; Security | Isolate affected principal and sessions; preserve audit; rotate scoped credentials; review grants, destinations and pending jobs; pause affected financial operations | Independent review of changes, revocation tests, reconciled pending operations and controlled access restoration |
| RB-005 Database/object restore | Corruption/loss or planned drill; SRE + domain owners | Freeze writes; select verified backup/checkpoints; isolated restore; match object versions/hashes; reapply revocations/holds; reconcile outbox/audit/provider facts and signature/transfer evidence | Measured RPO/RTO, tenant/security checks, financial/audit continuity, dual-reviewed reopening |
| RB-006 Release rollback | Canary/smoke/SLO/invariant regression; Release owner | Halt promotion; identify digest/schema compatibility; roll back compatible app or forward-fix schema; keep unknown provider actions pending; retain incident evidence | Read-only smoke plus affected invariants and reconciliation; no claim that app rollback reverses external funds |
| RB-007 Queue backlog/replay | Oldest age or dead-letter threshold exceeded; Platform | Identify poison payload/provider limit; isolate affected consumer; retain PostgreSQL intent; fix compatible handler; replay exact IDs under scoped service identity | Receipt/effect uniqueness, checkpoint progress, bounded drain and no revoked-scope disclosure |
| RB-008 Key/secret rotation | Scheduled expiry or suspected compromise; Security + SRE | Inventory dependent tasks/accounts; issue scoped replacement; overlap only documented signature rotation window; verify consumers; revoke old secret and audit | No exposed credentials, tested new signatures and rejection of retired credentials; provider unknowns reconciled |
| RB-009 Dispute/escalation | Participant objection or transfer/funding conflict; Deal operations | Record prior stage, evidence and contractual scope; hold unsent operations; reconcile already submitted provider actions; assign authorized reviewer; resolve through guarded recovery edges | Rights and money accounted for, immutable resolution and participant-visible safe outcome |

Phase 1 exercises dependency/restart and deployment harness procedures in local/nonproduction environments. Feature phases supply working scoped commands and sandbox provider details before accepting their integration. Phase 15 exercises restore, incident escalation, rollback, secret rotation and financial reconciliation with measured evidence and an accountable on-call rota. A runbook title or successful HTTP health response is not recovery proof.
