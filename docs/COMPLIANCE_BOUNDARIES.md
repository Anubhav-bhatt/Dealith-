# Compliance and capability boundaries

Status: Phase 0 architecture, 2026-09-07. Source: master §§7–9, 45, 75–77, 80, 95–98. This records engineering gates and review responsibilities, not a conclusion that a jurisdiction, provider or transaction is legally supported. No launch jurisdiction or provider is selected.

## Capability evaluation

An executable operation requires the intersection of environment enablement, approved DealTypePolicy version, eligible represented parties, reviewed jurisdiction/corridor, current verification and risk decisions, agreement authority and tested provider capability. Unknown or expired evidence denies the action with a safe reason. Client flags, purchased plans, investor intent and AI analysis cannot override a deny. Record inputs, policy version, approver evidence, decision and expiry for sensitive operations.

| Capability | Current boundary | Required owner and enablement evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Public commercial discovery | Planned approved listings; claims distinct from verified evidence | Product/Compliance: publication rules, prohibited listings, ownership/moderation and privacy review before exposure |
| Commercial acquisition/code/IP/license execution | Phase 11–12 provider-managed fiat escrow sandbox | Legal/Compliance + Settlement: permitted corridor, transferable rights, approved agreements, provider contract, exact funding/refund/dispute/reconciliation capability |
| Partnerships/revenue-share/other structures | Representable; execution disabled until a complete policy exists | Product/Legal + Architecture: scope, ongoing obligations, accounting/settlement and transfer model; tested ADR amendment |
| Equity/regulated investment | Default-off execution | Qualified jurisdiction reviewers and accountable operator: product classification, permitted parties/activities, required approvals/disclosures and provider support |
| Convertible instruments | Future; no executable policy | Separate scoped decision and complete tested requirements |
| Crypto | Future rail; default off; no Dealith custody, keys or seeds | [Crypto boundary](CRYPTO_BOUNDARY.md) plus jurisdiction/provider/asset/chain/precision/screening/refund review |
| Subscription billing | Phase 14, separate from acquisition money | Billing/Legal: plan terms, entitlements, invoices/tax treatment, cancellation, provider and privacy review |
| Production launch | Not authorized by architecture or sandbox success | Phase 15 complete release, legal, provider, security, recovery and operations evidence |

## Review inventory

Before real-money launch assign qualified reviewers for jurisdiction analysis; terms of service; privacy/cookie policy; seller/buyer agreements; marketplace/prohibited-listing rules; dispute/refund/escrow policy; applicable KYC/AML and sanctions design; tax/invoice obligations; investment and crypto analysis; provider/data-processing agreements; and retention schedules. Preserve signed/versioned review evidence and scope. Provider marketing or a generic payment API does not establish escrow suitability or permission for Dealith's business model.

Identity, business, ownership, repository, revenue and security verification are separate claims with evidence, reviewer, coverage and expiry. A badge must describe only its supported claim. Deterministic RiskDecision may hold sensitive actions and create an assigned case; unblocking requires authorized reviewed evidence. Staff cannot fabricate provider confirmation or mark an unverified claim true. Earlier phases introduce the minimum review controls before publishing, confidential disclosure or settlement; Phase 13 expands the console.

Privacy design minimizes collection by purpose and classification, records consent where applicable, and implements export/erasure with reauthentication and counterparty filtering. RetentionPolicy and LegalHold determine content retention without conferring access. Durable transaction provenance does not require unlimited raw KYC/source retention. The responsible reviewers choose retention/residency and deletion exceptions before collecting live sensitive data; policy unknown means the affected collection/export remains disabled.

Working-name clearance, customer-facing claims, promotion labeling and lawful notification preferences are also launch deliverables. The [risk register](PROJECT_RISK_REGISTER.md), [phase acceptance](PHASE_ACCEPTANCE_MATRIX.md) and [runbooks](RUNBOOKS.md) retain owners, triggers and evidence. No legal review, procurement approval or production sign-off is asserted in Phase 0.
