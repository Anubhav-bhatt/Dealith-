# Project risk register

Status: Phase 0 baseline, 2026-09-07. Sources: master §§91.3 and 97. Probability/impact are initial qualitative engineering estimates, not measured frequencies. Owners below are accountable roles; assign named people before the relevant phase begins. Every risk is OPEN until evidence closes it or a permitted non-blocking residual is actually accepted by an authorized owner. Listing a mitigation does not mean it exists.

Review weekly and at every phase exit. Record changed likelihood/impact, evidence, new trigger, assigned individual, mitigation progress and decision. A missing design for confidentiality, financial correctness or audit is a Phase 0 blocker; a deliberately disabled provider/jurisdiction capability with a complete interface/gate is a tracked future dependency. Unknown approval is never acceptance. [Threat model](THREAT_MODEL.md) supplies abuse cases; [phase acceptance](PHASE_ACCEPTANCE_MATRIX.md) and [runbooks](RUNBOOKS.md) provide gates and responses.
